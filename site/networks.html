Flow a state, not a network

What this is

Not a network but a state — a reconfiguration across networks when challenge meets skill. The threads show which couplings strengthen and which fall quiet, composited from the fMRI flow literature.

Hover or tap a region — coloured threads strengthen in flow, grey dashed threads fall quiet, and hollow markers power down.

Composite after Ulrich et al. (2014), Huskey et al. (2018) and Dietrich's transient-hypofrontality account (2004). Schematic: flow has no canonical connectome, and the couplings drawn here are illustrative rather than measured. Regions are placed for legibility around the ring, not by anatomical position — for that, see the Region Atlas.
